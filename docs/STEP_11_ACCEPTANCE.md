## Shared conditions for all functions
- Authenticate the actor and enforce patient/caregiver scope in the API; UI hiding alone is insufficient.
- Validate required fields on the server; show a clear error and do not save partial success.
- Persist the result across browser refresh and backend restart, and retain required audit/history records.
- Distinguish third-party request attempted, delivered, acknowledged and acted upon.
- Record timezone for schedules and event timestamps; display times in the patient’s selected timezone.
- Do not use real patient records or emergency requests in classroom tests.

## Acceptance evidence template
For each ID, attach to its Jira issue: input screenshot, successful output after reload, database/API evidence, one invalid-input result, one permission or integration-failure result where relevant, and the team member’s commit/PR link. Exact faculty additions and deployment requirements remain to be confirmed.
